a note from me for the people who made this real
an important update

i gotta be honest.

this is probably the hardest thing i’ve had to write about Blur.

01 · a note from me

Blur started as a small project i made because i wanted somewhere fun to build, watch stuff, play games, and hang out. Then u guys found it. Somehow it became a real little community, and i still don’t think i’ve fully processed that.

02 · why this is happening

The site grew way faster than i expected, and with that came risks i can’t just pretend aren’t there. The chance of getting in serious trouble is too high for me to keep running Blur the same way. I hate that this is where things are, but keeping everyone safe has to matter more than keeping the site online.

03 · what this means

For now, Blur may be paused or shut down while i figure out what’s actually responsible to do next. This isn’t me being mad at anyone, and it isn’t because the community did something wrong. I’m just trying to make the safest choice before the situation gets worse.

04 · what i’ll remember

I’ll remember the messages, movie nights, games, playlists, bug reports, and all the random jokes that only made sense here. That stuff wasn’t just website activity to me. It proved that something i made could actually mean something to people.

thanks for giving Blur a life. — Grayson
whatever happens next, i’ll remember that u were here. ♥
for the people who helped hold this together a private thank-you
mods

this part is for u.

i didn’t want to leave without saying this properly.

thank you, seriously

U gave up your time to help make Blur feel like a real place. U answered questions, dealt with problems, kept conversations moving, and looked out for people even when nobody was watching. I know that work can be invisible a lot of the time, but I saw it. I’m genuinely grateful for every bit of it.

none of this was expected

When I started Blur, I never imagined I’d have people helping me take care of it. That still means more than I’m probably able to explain without sounding cheesy.

whatever happens next

Please don’t feel like any of this was your fault or your responsibility to fix. You already did more than enough. I hope u know that.

Thank you for believing in something that was still being figured out.

— Grayson
